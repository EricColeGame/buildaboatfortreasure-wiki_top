## What Build a Boat for Treasure Actually Is

## The Core Loop: Build, Sail, Collect, Repeat

The premise is exactly what the title says. You spawn on a build plot, assemble a ship out of blocks, then launch it into a course full of obstacles. Success is measured in distance traveled and gold earned. The further you get, the more gold you collect, and gold is the resource that drives everything else.

That loop is the entire game, and it is why it has lasted nearly a decade. It is a design sandbox wrapped around a survival challenge. There is no story to finish. There is only the next stage and the next rebuild.

## Design Reasoning: How Players Think About Boats

Everything in this section is **player reasoning**, not documented statistics. Chillz Studios has not published a block list, a weight table, or a buoyancy formula. So treat these as mental models, not rules.

**Buoyancy comes first.** A boat that sinks is not a boat. In practice, players tend to keep the hull wide and the mass low. Stacking heavy material high above the waterline is a common way to build something that looks impressive on the plot and immediately turtles on launch.

**Thrust is a trade-off.** More propulsion usually means more speed, and more speed usually means less time to react to what is coming. Beginners often overshoot on thrust and undershoot on control.

**Protection is about where, not how much.** Armor placed on the front of the hull does different work than armor placed over the top. Players frequently discover that the obstacle that killed them last run was coming from an angle they had not considered.

**Redundancy helps.** A single propulsion unit is a single point of failure. Spreading function across the build means one bad hit does not end the run. This is a design principle, not a documented mechanic.

The honest summary: nobody can hand you a blueprint that guarantees success, because the course and the physics interactions are what they are. Iteration is the actual strategy.

## Earning Gold and Whether Group Perks Are Worth It

Gold accumulates as you progress, and the official page confirms that group membership increases gold gain by **25%** at every tier. That is the one benefit shared across all three levels.

The tiers diverge on health and, for Royal Member, on **+4 barrels of TNT**. Health is self-explanatory. TNT is not — the official description does not explain how it is used, whether it is consumed, or where it appears in the build process. If you want to know, join the group at that tier and observe it yourself in-game. Do not trust a third-party guide that claims to know the exact mechanics, because the developer has not said.

Whether the perks are "worth it" depends entirely on how much you play. A 25% gold boost compounds over hundreds of runs. It is worth very little over five.
