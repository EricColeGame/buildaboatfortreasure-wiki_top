## Design Reasoning: Buoyancy, Weight, and Thrust

The following is player reasoning, not documented stats. No official block names, shop prices, or blueprint lists are reproduced here because the sources do not support them.

Think of your boat as three competing budgets.

**Buoyancy versus weight.** Every block adds mass. Blocks that float or displace water well give you lift. The classic mistake is stacking heavy material high on the hull, which raises the center of gravity and makes the whole craft roll on the first turn. Keep the heaviest parts low and centered, and build upward only as far as you need.

**Thrust versus drag.** A boat needs forward force and a shape that does not fight the water. Wide, flat fronts catch waves and ramps badly. Narrow, angled fronts deflect them. If your craft spins instead of driving forward, your thrust is probably offset from your center of mass rather than weak.

**Protection versus cost.** Armor, barriers, and hull reinforcement all add weight. The advertised health multipliers tell you that durability is a legitimate strategy, but a boat that survives every hit and never reaches the goal still earns nothing.

A useful iteration habit: change one variable at a time. Add thrust, test. Move weight, test. If you change five things at once, you learn nothing about which one mattered.

## Practical Habits That Reduce Rebuild Time

- **Test on the earliest stages.** Early obstacles are cheap failure modes. A design that fails there will fail harder later.
- **Save your work.** If the game offers a save or blueprint feature, use it. Verifying whether it exists is as simple as looking at the build plot UI.
- **Build for the stage you are stuck on.** Some obstacles punish tall builds, others punish wide ones. Rebuilding for a specific problem beats one universal design.
- **Watch other players in your server.** The listed server size is small — seven players — so a public server is a reasonable place to observe successful designs in action.
- **Consider group membership only if the perks match your problem.** Gold bonuses help if you are farming. Health bonuses help if you are dying. Decide based on which wall you keep hitting.
