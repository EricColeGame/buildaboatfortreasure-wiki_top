## The Core Loop: Build, Then Sail

The premise in the title is accurate. You get a build plot, you assemble a ship out of blocks, and then you send that ship into an obstacle-filled course. Surviving the run earns gold. That is the loop, and it has been stable enough to sustain the game for nearly a decade.

The official description does not break down the stages, list the obstacles, or publish a catalog of blocks. If you see a guide claiming an exact stage count or a precise gold payout per run, treat it as a third-party observation rather than developer documentation. Stage layouts change over time, and community guides age quickly.

What the official page *does* advertise is membership perks. These are documented, so they are safe to plan around:

- **Group member:** 25% more gold.
- **Mega Member:** 300% more health and 25% more gold.
- **Royal Member:** +4 barrels of TNT, 300% more health, and 25% more gold.

Notice what these bonuses actually do. Two of the three tiers boost health, which suggests survivability is a real constraint on how far a run goes. The gold multipliers reward the same runs you were already doing. The Royal tier's extra TNT is the most unusual entry — it implies explosives are a resource you spend, not just a hazard you avoid. Beyond that, the official page does not explain how TNT is used in a run, so do not assume a specific mechanic.

## Design Reasoning That Holds Up

This part is player reasoning, not documented fact. Nobody at Chillz Studios published a build guide, and any article that pretends otherwise is inventing authority it does not have. Still, a few principles are close to universal in physics-driven boat builders:

**Buoyancy beats size.** A wide, light hull tends to float more predictably than a tall, heavy one. If your ship sinks on the launch, that is a weight-and-displacement problem, not a materials problem.

**Thrust and steering are separate concerns.** Being able to move forward does not mean you can avoid anything. Builds that survive longer stages usually have some way to change direction under load.

**Protection trades against speed.** Armor helps against impacts, but every block adds mass. The interesting decisions in this game are almost always about that trade-off, not about finding a single correct block.

**Expect to rebuild.** The loop rewards iteration. A design that fails at the same spot three times is telling you something specific about where its weakness is.

If you want to know which blocks exist and what they cost, open the game and look at the build UI directly. That is faster and more reliable than any secondhand list.

**"It's the same as the app."** It is not. The mobile app with a similar name is a separate product with a separate developer. Progress, currency, and content do not transfer.
