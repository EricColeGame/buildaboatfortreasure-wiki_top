## What Counts as an "Item" in Build a Boat for Treasure

The word "item" gets used loosely in this game, and that's the first thing to untangle. When players ask about all items, they usually mean three overlapping groups: structural blocks that form the hull, functional parts that make the boat move or survive, and reward gear that only shows up from chests or milestones.

Structural blocks are the foundation. Nothing else works without them, because every sail, thruster, and cannon has to be attached to something solid. Functional parts are where the strategy lives — propulsion, lift, steering, and utility. Reward gear sits on top: rarer parts and cosmetics that are fun to own but rarely carry a run on their own.

One important caveat: the developer adds, rebalances, and occasionally retires parts, so treat any catalog as a snapshot. Player experience suggests that the broad categories stay stable even when individual names and numbers change.

## How You Unlock Items: Shop, Chests, and Stage Rewards

There is no single store where you buy the entire catalog. Community reports consistently describe four routes into your inventory, and each one rewards a different play style.

The **starting inventory** gives you the basics — enough blocks and a couple of core parts to build a first boat. **Gold earned from stages** feeds the in-game shop, which is the most predictable path to mid-tier functional items. **Chest drops** after clearing stages are random, which is why two players with similar progress can have very different inventories.

**Milestone rewards** for reaching certain distances or clearing specific stages tend to hand out the strongest parts. These are the items worth grinding for, because they usually solve a problem that blocks a lot of players.

| Source | How it works | Effort | What you typically get |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starting inventory | Available from your first session | None | Basic blocks and a few core parts |
| Gold shop | Spend gold earned by clearing stages | Low to medium | Mid-tier functional parts |

| Milestone rewards | Reach set distances or clear key stages | High | Stronger, rarer parts |
| Limited-time events | Available only during a seasonal window | Variable | Cosmetics and novelty gear |

A practical tip: don't spend every coin the moment you can afford something. Save for the part that solves your current wall. If you keep sinking at the same stage, the answer is almost always one specific item, not five random ones.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many items are there in Build a Boat for Treasure?**

There's no fixed number, because the catalog grows with updates. Player experience suggests the practical count sits in the dozens once you include structural blocks, functional parts, and reward gear — and it changes with nearly every major patch. Treat any exact figure as outdated quickly.

**Do you have to buy every item with gold?**

No. Gold covers shop inventory, but chest drops, milestone rewards, and limited-time events supply a large share of the build a boat for treasure all items catalog. Some of the strongest parts are earned rather than purchased.

**What's the best first item to unlock?**

Something that improves propulsion. Nearly every early wall in the game is a speed or clearance problem, and a better engine or a set of balloons unlocks more stages than armor or cosmetics will.

**Are tier lists worth following?**

They're useful as a rough filter, not as a rulebook. Item balance shifts, and a part that's mediocre in general can be exactly what a specific stage requires. Use community rankings to narrow your shortlist, then test in-game.

**Can you lose items once you've unlocked them?**

Unlocked inventory is generally persistent — you don't lose parts permanently when a boat is destroyed. You lose the build, not the collection, which is why rebuilding and retrying costs time rather than progress.

The short version: learn the categories, respect the acquisition routes, and match items to hazards instead of chasing whatever looks strongest on paper. That approach will carry you further than any single item ever will.
