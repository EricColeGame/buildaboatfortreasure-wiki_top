## Build Reasoning Players Commonly Share

Everything in this section is player reasoning, not developer documentation. The official sources do not describe block properties, physics values, or optimal designs. But the design logic below follows directly from the two verbs in the description: build and sail.

**Buoyancy and weight.** A boat floats or it does not. Piling on heavy protection without enough displacement tends to sink a build, and adding buoyant material without structure tends to make it flimsy. Most experienced players describe finding a balance rather than maximizing any single trait.

**Thrust and control.** Moving forward is not the same as moving precisely. Obstacles reward the ability to stop, adjust, and re-aim, so builds that are fast but uncontrollable often stall out. Redundancy helps here: if one propulsion source is knocked away, a second one can keep the run alive.

**Protection placement.** Armor on the front does nothing for a hit from above or behind. Players who run stages repeatedly tend to protect the parts they cannot afford to lose, and accept losses elsewhere.

**Iteration over perfection.** Because gold comes from sailing, not from planning, the practical loop is build, launch, observe what broke, and rebuild. Small revisions beat total redesigns.

Also note the +4 barrels of TNT listed for Royal Members. In a stage-based obstacle game, TNT is generally read as an offensive or demolition tool, but no official source explains its exact role, so verify in game before building a strategy around it.
