## How to Find and Join the Official Server Safely

The single biggest mistake new players make is clicking the first invite link they see in a video description or comment. Impersonator servers are common in every large Roblox community, and they exist for one reason: to farm attention, accounts, or items. Always start from a source the developer controls.

| Step | What to do | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the game's official Roblox page | Social links are posted by the developer, not a stranger |
| 2 | Check the developer's Roblox group page | The group's social links tab is the canonical source |
| 3 | Compare the server name, icon, and history | Real servers have a long announcement backlog |
| 4 | Read the rules channel before posting | Most removals come from ignoring pinned rules |
| 5 | Set your nickname to match your Roblox username | Many servers require it for verification |
| 6 | Pick your roles in the role menu | Roles unlock build, trade, and group-finding channels |

A good starting point is [the game's official Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/537413528/Build-a-Boat-for-Treasure), where the developer's own links are listed. If a server asks for your Roblox password, a login through a third-party site, or a "verification" that involves your account details, close it immediately — no legitimate build a boat for treasure official discord will ever ask for that.

### Red flags that mean "leave now"

- Direct messages offering free gold, free boats, or rare blocks in exchange for a click.
- Invites posted by accounts with no post history and a name full of numbers.
- Servers that hide their member list, lock every channel, and push you toward a single "claim" link.
- Anyone asking you to trade items outside the game's own systems.

## Etiquette and Safety Rules Worth Following

Communities stay useful only when people follow a few unwritten rules. These are the ones that come up again and again in player experience discussions.

| Rule of thumb | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Search before posting | Duplicate questions bury good answers |
| Post in the right channel | Keeps each area readable |
| Avoid spoiling late stages in general chat | Some players want to discover them |
| Don't spam trade offers | Trade channels usually have cooldowns |
| Credit the original builder | Reposting someone's design without credit gets removed |
| Keep bug reports reproducible | "It broke" cannot be fixed; steps can |

On the safety side, treat your Roblox account like a bank account. Turn on two-factor authentication, never share a login, and be skeptical of anyone who is unusually generous in a direct message. Scammers in gaming communities almost always open with a gift before they ask for something.

## FAQ

**Is there an official Build a Boat for Treasure Discord server?**
Yes. The development team maintains an official community server, and the safest way to reach it is through the links posted on the game's Roblox page or the developer's group page. Avoid invites from random comments or direct messages, since impersonator servers are common.

**How can I tell whether I joined the real server?**
Look for a long history of announcements, an active staff team with visible roles, a rules channel that matches the game's actual content, and a member list that is not hidden. If every channel is locked and you are pushed toward one "claim" link, you are in the wrong place.

**Do I need to be in the build a boat for treasure official discord to get better at the game?**
No, but it compresses the learning curve. Stage advice, build feedback, and patch news arrive faster in a moderated community than they do through trial and error, especially after an update changes how a design behaves.

**What should I do if I spot a scam in the community?**
Do not engage with the account. Report the message or user through the platform's own reporting tools, and if it happened inside the server, notify a moderator with a screenshot. Never send items, account details, or payment information to resolve a "problem" someone claims you have.
